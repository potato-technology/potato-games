How to play

↔You bounce on your own! Steer with ←→ or AD, or just move the mouse. On a phone, hold your finger where you want to go.
★Shoot star shots with Space, ↑ or a click (tap the star button on a phone). They pop monsters above you.
👟Land on top of a monster to stomp it. Bumping into one from below knocks you out! Spiky ones can't be stomped.
🧱Blue platforms slide, purple ones float up and down, brown ones crumble, clouds vanish after one bounce and pink ones hop away.
🚀Grab springs, trampolines, jetpacks, propeller hats, shields and magnets. Go past the edge and you pop out the other side.
⛈Watch out for the Storm Cloud boss! Bounce off a spring to bump it from below, or shoot it, to clear the sky.

P pauses, M mutes.
