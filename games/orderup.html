How to play
  • Click to grab, click to put down. Bun on a plate, raw patty on the grill, cooked patty on the bun, then toppings.
  • Watch the grill! A green check means it's cooked. Leave it too long and it burns.
  • Serve it: grab the finished plate (or fries, soda or milkshake) and click the customer.
  • Combos: serve people back-to-back while they're happy to multiply your tips.
  • Milkshakes (from day 7): tap the blender, then serve them before they melt.
  • Lunch Rush: no clock and 3 hearts. Every angry customer costs a heart, and every 8 happy ones win one back.
  • Chef ranks: every customer you serve counts. Rank up for bigger tips and more patient customers.
  • Keys: P pauses, M mutes, Esc drops what you're holding.
  • Earn 3 stars each day, then spend your cash and stars on a better kitchen and a fancier diner.
