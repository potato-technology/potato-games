How to play
  • Click a stone pad to build a tower. Each tower is good at something different.
  • Press Start (or Space) to send the goblins. Your towers shoot all by themselves!
  • Every enemy you stop gives gold. Spend it on more towers, or click a tower to upgrade it.
  • Each goblin that gets through the gate costs a life. Keep 18+ lives for 3 stars.
  • Spend your stars on Upgrades to make every tower stronger.
  • Sir Pip the knight (from level 2): click him or press H, then click the road to send him into battle. He gets stronger as he fights.
  • Treasure chests pop up near the road. Click them fast for gold or a spell recharge!
  • Watch out for goblin balloons (pop them and two goblins drop out) and shield goblins (magic towers smash shields).
  • Keys: F changes the speed (1x, 2x, 3x), P pauses, M mutes. The little pictures by the Next button show who's coming next.
