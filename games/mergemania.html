  • Swipe or press the arrow keys to slide every tile at once.
  • Two matching tiles that bump together merge into the next one: eggs hatch into chicks, chicks grow into birds, birds become dragons!
  • After each slide a new egg appears. Don't let the board fill up!
  • Rocks block the way. Merge right next to a rock to crack it.
  • Merge on every slide to build a combo chain (up to x4 points). A rainbow Wild Star merges with anything!
  • Stuck? Use Undo (Z), the Hammer (H) to smash a tile, or Swap (X) two tiles.
