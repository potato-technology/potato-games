How to ride

↔Steer with ←→ or AD (or point with the mouse). Hold ↓ to tuck for speed and ↑ to brake.
⤴Space (or a click) does a little hop. Use it to jump over logs and ice cracks!
🌀In the air: ←→ spin, ↑ flip, ↓ grab. Land straight or you'll wipe out! Let go early and the board finishes the spin for you.
🛤Ride straight onto rails to grind them. Chain tricks quickly for a combo.
🚩In slalom, pass between the two flags of every gate. Each missed gate adds 3 seconds.
❄Every event hides 3 snowflakes, some floating in the air. Collect them to unlock special gear.
📱On a phone: hold the left or right side to steer, the middle to tuck or grab, and swipe up to jump or flip.

P pauses, M mutes.
