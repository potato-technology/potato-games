WAVE RIDER

Jet ski racing! Slalom the buoys, fly off ramps and pull wild tricks.

How to Ride

  • Drive: hold ↑ or W. Steer with ← → (or A D). ↓ brakes.
  • Buoys: go around each buoy the way its arrow points. Red = pass on its left, yellow = pass on its right. Each good pass adds Power (more top speed). Miss one and your power drops to zero!
  • Ramps: while you're in the air press ←/→ to spin, ↑ to front flip, ↓ to backflip. Land before you finish a trick and you wipe out!
  • Each landed trick gives a Turbo. Use it with Space or Shift. Boost gates give a free burst too.
  • Crossing other riders' wakes and waves makes you hop. Watch out for rocks, logs and boats!
  • P pause, M mute, C camera. On a phone the gas is automatic: use the buttons to steer.
