How to play

  • Numbers next to each row and on top of each column say how many squares in a row are filled.
  • "3 1" means a group of 3, then at least one empty gap, then a group of 1.
  • Click or drag to fill squares. Switch to the ✕ tool (or right-click) to mark squares you know are empty.
  • Start with big numbers: a 5 in a row of 5 fills the whole row!
  • Fill every right square to reveal a colourful picture. It goes into your Album.
  • Stuck? Press 💡 Hint. In Kid mode, wrong squares just bounce back.
  • Keys: arrows move, Space fills, X marks, H hint, Z undo, P pause, M mute.
