How to Play

  • Drag cards to move them, or click a card to send it to the best spot.
  • Klondike: build down in alternating colours (red on black). Only a King can go in an empty column. Move every card to the four foundations, Ace to King.
  • Spider: build down by one in any suit, but you can only move a group if it's all the same suit. A full King-to-Ace run of one suit clears away. Click the stock to deal a new row (every column needs a card first).
  • Pyramid: remove two uncovered cards that add up to 13 (Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13 on its own). Clear the whole pyramid to win!
  • Stars: win (★), beat the target time (★) and win without Undo (★). Stars build your castle!
  • Keys: Space draw, U undo, H hint, N new game, P pause, M mute.
