TOW RUSH

Cars are breaking down all over the city. Get there before the other tow trucks do.

  • Race to the call. Every second you take, the job pays less.
  • Beat the rivals. Other tow companies head out a few seconds after the call. If they get there first, you lose the job.
  • Watch the traffic. Crashing costs you repair money.
  • Follow the rules when cops are around. Run a red light, speed past 80 km/h, drive on the wrong side, or smash into cars where police can see, and they'll chase you. Get caught and you sit in jail for 30 seconds while the clock keeps running.
  • Hook it and haul it back to your yard to get paid.
  • Upgrade your truck. Your money goes into the bank after every shift. Spend it in the garage on a faster engine, better tires, a bigger nitro tank, and a quicker hook. Arrested? Press B to pay bail and get out early.
  • Drift! Going fast, hold a direction and tap Space to kick the back out. Steer into the turn for a bigger angle, countersteer to straighten up. Longer drifts build a combo. Chain them back to back, and don't crash. Drifts pay cash and fill your nitro.
