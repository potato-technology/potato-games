Sunny AcresGrow crops, raise animals and fill truck orders before sunset!

Day 1Level 10 coins0 ★
  • Click a grassy plot to till it, then click the soil to plant. Bare Hands pick the seed your orders need, or choose a seed bag yourself (3–0).
  • Water with the can (2) so crops grow faster, and refill it at the pond. Harvest when they sparkle! Hold and drag to do a whole row.
  • Feed animals 1 wheat each to get eggs, milk, wool and cheese, then click to collect. A beehive makes honey all by itself!
  • Fill truck orders for coins and XP. Rush orders pay extra, quick harvests make combos, and shiny golden crops give bonus coins!
  • Shake the apple trees for apples, bake bread and apple pies in the barn oven, and win blue ribbons on County Fair days!
  • Keys: ←↑↓→ or WASD move the cursor, Space uses your tool, Q/E switch tools, P pauses, M mutes.
  • Hit 3 goals for 3 stars a day. Level up to unlock crops, animals and goodies in the Market.
