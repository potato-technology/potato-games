Tater Typer

The great potato race is on! Type fast to roll your potato to the finish line.

  • Type the fun facts exactly as they're written: potatoes, food, animals, space and more. Every right letter rolls your potato forward, and you'll learn something too!
  • Wrong letter? Your potato trips and won't move until you type the right one.
  • Butter boost: type 25 letters in a row with no mistakes to throw a pat of butter. The racer closest to you slips and stops for a moment.
  • Endless: the sentences never stop, and a giant potato masher chases you, getting faster every few seconds. See how far you can roll before you get mashed!
  • Race a friend: one of you makes a race and gets a code, the other types the code in to join.
  • Win Spuds for your place or distance, plus a bonus for typing fast.
