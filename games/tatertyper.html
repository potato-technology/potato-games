How to play
  • Type the fun facts exactly as they're written. Every right letter rolls your potato forward, and you'll learn something too!
  • Wrong letter? Your potato trips and won't move until you type the right one.
  • Golden words shine in the sentence. Type one with no mistakes to open a mystery power: Turbo types the next word for you, Freeze stops everyone else, Butter makes a rival slip, and a Shield saves you from your next mistake.
  • Butter boost: 25 letters in a row with no mistakes throws butter at the racer closest to you.
  • Spud Cup: three races in a row for points. The final has a boss, King Russet, who throws butter back!
  • Endless: a giant potato masher chases you, getting faster all the time. How far can you roll?
  • Daily Dash: a new challenge every day. Keep your daily streak going!
  • Level up (1 XP per letter) to unlock sneakers and trails, and earn badges in the Locker.
