How to play
  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click where you want to go. Hold Shift to sneak quietly.
  • Guards see you in their flashlight beams, and from far away if you stand in lamp light. Stay in the shadows!
  • Press E or right-click to throw a coin. Guards go check the noise. Space drops a smoke bomb.
  • Grab the treasure, then reach the green EXIT. Watch out for cameras, lasers and squeaky floor plates!
  • Tiptoe right up behind a calm guard to swipe his pocket watch. Guard pups sniff you out, but they love shiny coins!
  • Step onto an air vent grate to crawl to a secret exit. Try the Daily Heist for a new twist every day!
  • Earn 3 stars: escape, grab all the bonus loot, and never get spotted. Spend gold on gadgets at the Hideout.
