How to dig

  • Click sand to dig. Your first dig is always safe!
  • A number tells how many crabs hide in the 8 squares touching it. A blank spot means no crabs nearby, so the sand around it opens up by itself.
  • Right-click (or hold your finger, or switch to the 🚩 tool) to plant a flag where you think a crab is.
  • Click a number when all its crabs are flagged to dig all around it at once.
  • Dig up a crab and it pinches you! You lose a heart. Run out of hearts and the tide comes in.
  • Stuck? Use the 🧭 compass and Polly will point to a safe spot.
  • Find coins, gems and a treasure chest as you dig. Spend gold in the shop!
  • Keys: arrows move, Space digs, F flags, C compass, P pause, M mute.
