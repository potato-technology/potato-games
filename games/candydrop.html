How to play

✂️Swipe across a rope to cut it. Drop the candy into Munch's mouth!
⭐Catch all 3 stars with the candy on the way down.
🪝Dotted circles grab the candy with a new rope when it swings close.
🫧Bubbles float the candy up. Tap the candy to pop the bubble.
💨Tap an air cushion to puff the candy the way it points (or press 1–4). Space pops a bubble.
🌀Teleport pipes zap the candy to the matching pipe. Bouncy pads bounce it. Spikes break it!
💡Stuck? Use a hint to see the path. R restarts, P pauses, M mutes.
