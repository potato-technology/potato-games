How to play

  • Flippers: ← / Z and → / /, or tap the left and right side of the screen.
  • Launch: hold ↓ or Space (or touch and hold) to pull the plunger, then let go. A soft launch can drop into the lit top lane for a Skill Shot!
  • Nudge: N / ↑ or the NUDGE button bumps the table. Too many nudges and you TILT.
  • Multiball: knock down all 3 drop targets to light the lock, then shoot the ball into the lock hole twice.
  • Missions: shoot the hole when no lock is lit to start a mission. Finish all 6 for Party Mode!
  • Top lanes raise your bonus multiplier and light the kickback in the left outlane.
