  • Slide the paddle with the mouse (or drag with a finger), or use ← → / A D.
  • Launch the ball with Space or a click. Hit it with the paddle's edges to aim sideways!
  • Catch power-ups: multi-ball, wide paddle, lasers (fire with Space), fireball, slow ball, sticky paddle, shield and extra lives. Dodge the grey ones!
  • Break lots of bricks without touching the paddle for a combo. Blast explosive bricks for chain reactions!
  • Smashing bricks fills your BLITZ meter. When it's full, press B (or tap BLITZ) to fire a shockwave up every column!
  • Beat each world's boss for a trophy, and try Brick Rain: an endless mode where new rows keep falling.
  • Earn up to 3 stars per level, catch coins, and spend them on upgrades, paddle skins and ball trails. P pauses, M mutes.
