SUMO BOTS

Dash, charge and block. Be the last robot on the platform!

How to Play

Push every other robot off the floating platform. The last bot standing wins the round. Win 2 rounds to win the match!

💨DashTap to zoom forward. Beats bots that aren't guarding.
🛡️BlockHold to brace. Bounces a dash right back!
💥ChargeHold Dash, then let go. Smashes through Block!
  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Dash: tap Space (or J). Charge: hold it, then let go.
  • Block: hold Shift (or K). It uses energy, so don't hold it forever!
  • Hitting a bot while it charges interrupts it. Get on the inside and push them out!
  • Near the edge you wobble. Steer back in fast to save yourself.
  • Grab power-ups: 🔩 Heavy, ⚡ Turbo, 🫧 Bubble (saves one fall) and 💫 Shockwave.
  • P pauses, M mutes. On a phone, use the stick and the buttons.
